Volkai Rivals Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Volkai Rivals processes, why, and the choices you have. Volkai Rivals is developed by Felipe Lorca, Chile, who is responsible for your data. Contact: volkairivals@gmail.com.

1. Data we process

  • Your account identifier. The first time you open the game, a guest account is created with a random identifier stored securely on your device. We do not use your device's advertising identifier.
  • Game data. Your player ID, player name, card collection, decks, story progress, missions, achievements, mastery, trophies and match results, coins, jades and packs.
  • Purchases. Real-money payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive the purchase confirmation to deliver what you bought; we never see your payment details.
  • Friends. The IDs of the players you add as friends and friendly-duel invitations.
  • Reports and moderation. Reports you send or that other players send about you (who reported, the reason and the date).
  • Sign in with Apple or Google, only if you choose to link your account to recover it: the identifier that account provides to the game.
  • Technical data. Our provider records the IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country or city) when you sign in, and our servers keep technical logs (request identifiers and errors) to run the game and investigate failures.

We do not ask for or collect your contacts, precise location, photos or microphone. The game shows no advertising, does not sell your data and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and save your progress.
  • To play online matches, match you with other players and calculate trophies and rewards.
  • To deliver the purchases, rewards and packs you are entitled to.
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and to moderate reported names and behavior.
  • To support you and improve the game's stability.

We process this data because it is necessary to provide the game you request, because of our legitimate interest in keeping it safe and fair, and where the law requires it.

3. Who we share it with

  • Providers that process it on our behalf: Microsoft (Azure PlayFab, Azure Functions and Azure Cosmos DB), which hosts accounts, progress and online matches.
  • Apple and Google, for the stores, payments and sign-in, under their own privacy policies.
  • Other players see your player name, guardian, skin, arena and trophies during matches, and your player ID if you share it or they add you as a friend.

We do not sell or rent your data.

4. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on Microsoft servers located in the United States. By using the game you agree to this international transfer, made with providers that apply appropriate security measures.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete it, your account and its game data, recorded purchases, friends and linkages are deleted; the provider may take a few days to complete the deletion. Finished match records are deleted automatically and technical logs are kept for a limited time (up to 90 days). We may keep what the law requires.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your account at any time from the game: Settings › Account › Delete account, or by writing to us.
  • Access your data or ask for a copy, correct it (your name can be changed in your Profile) and object to certain uses by writing to volkairivals@gmail.com.

We reply within a reasonable time, in accordance with Chilean personal data protection law.

7. Children

Volkai Rivals is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

The game's communications are encrypted (HTTPS), your account identifier is kept in your device's secure storage, and rewards and purchases are decided by the server. No system is perfect, but we apply reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will update the date above; important changes will also be announced in the game.

10. Contact
